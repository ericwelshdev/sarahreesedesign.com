A few things I'm asked as a creative

Who am I?
Started my creative passion in my early years with mediums like photography and sketching. I've been lucky enough to have worked with startups and big brands alike - from independent boutique shops to Fortune 500 companies. Experience in most creative areas - front end development to brand creation. My goal is to develop partnerships with brands to deliver strategic and emotionally-resonant campaigns that successfully achieve brand goals.
Where do you find inspiration?
Everywhere. Grocery store aisles (can we talk about all those craft beer labels?), surrounding myself with inspirational people, and immersing myself in nature. The best part of being a creative is constantly learning and improving skills. For me, that never gets old.
What professional tools do you use?
All Apple hardware. Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, XD, Acrobat, After Affects, Lightroom) - MS Office (Outlook, Word, PowerPoint, and more), Figma, Sketch, InVision, any design tools you want to throw at me! Extensive print knowledge/designing for print. HTML & CSS, UX/UI & prototyping tools. Plenty of project management tools, as well!
What is your specialty?
I believe, given my diverse creative background, my specialty is curiosity. I don't know something? I dive in! My favorite? Editorial design. There's something about those minimal crispy clean pages.
